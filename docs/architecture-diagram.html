Every case the system handles
Trade allowed EXECUTED
Idea passes all rules → order sent to Perpl → green receipt. Shows in your feed & the leaderboard.
Trade blocked REJECTED
Breaks a rule (too big, wrong market, too risky, low confidence) → refused & still receipted, so refusals are provable too.
Paper trade SHADOW
Otter/Owl practice without real money. Logged on-chain before the outcome, so track records can’t be faked later.
Ask for slack
On a strong idea that just exceeds a limit, the trader asks you for a narrow, expiring exception. You approve with Face ID.
Referee hits the brakes PAUSED
Weekly loss hit, liquidation too close, or data stale 3× → the watchdog pauses on-chain. Only you can resume.
You pull the leash PAUSED
Hold the button + Face ID → everything stops instantly: loop off, key disabled, policy denies all.
AI confused / attacked HOLD
Bad or injected AI output can’t parse → treated as HOLD, receipted as rejected. The AI never executes directly.
Spot swap (Kuru)
Owl hedges / you swap AUSD↔MON on the Kuru order book, user-signed.
